# 🏛️ Pantheon — Official Ruleset

> **Version:** 2.0
> **Supersedes:** Chaty Trophy System Rules v1.1
> **Model:** Pantheon Model of Relevant Completion

---

## 🎯 Philosophy

Pantheon exists to turn a trophy list into a record of the player's journey.

Trophies should push the player to explore the campaign, the systems, the meaningful side content and the challenges that actually define a game.

Earning the Platinum should mean the player **lived everything worth living in that game**.

The system does not chase absolute 100%. Repetitive content, excessive grinding and tasks with no meaningful value to the experience can be left out.

---

## 🚪 Scope and Entry Gate

Pantheon builds lists for **games that lack a native achievement system on the target platform**, on any platform: Nintendo Switch and Switch 2, retro and emulated titles, DRM-free releases without achievements, mobile games, older PlayStation and Xbox titles, and anything else in that situation.

Before any list is created, the entry gate must be checked:

1. Identify the **target platform** the player owns.
2. Determine whether that platform's version has native achievements or trophies.
3. If it does, **no list is created**. Explain that the native list already covers it.

The gate is evaluated per **game + platform pair**, never per game alone. A title can have Steam achievements and no Switch equivalent — that is a valid Pantheon candidate on Switch and an invalid one on Steam.

### Two build modes

| Mode | Condition | Primary risk |
|---|---|---|
| 🌱 **Genesis** | No platform has native achievements | Factual accuracy — everything comes from research |
| 🔁 **Port** | Another platform has a native list | Content drift — the source list may reference content the target version lacks |

Port Mode is preferred whenever available. A native achievement list is verified ground truth and removes the largest source of error in list creation.

## 📜 Official Rules

### Rule 1 — Every trophy must be verifiable

When an objective is completed, there must be an objective way to confirm the condition was met:

- A completion screen.
- An item obtained.
- A quest marked complete.
- A recorded percentage.
- A boss defeated.
- A rank achieved.
- A save-file record.
- Information available in in-game menus or statistics.

The player must never be asked to manually track statistics the game itself does not record.

---

### Rule 2 — No imaginary counters

Cumulative objectives are not created when the game offers no reliable way to track progress.

**Invalid:** *Defeat 500 enemies* — when the game has no enemy-defeat statistic.

**Valid:** *Complete 50 challenges* — when the game clearly records how many challenges were completed.

---

### Rule 3 — The list must represent the game well

The list should encourage a broad, representative experience. Where relevant, consider:

main campaign, exploration, side content, important systems and mechanics, optional bosses, secret areas, collectibles, minigames, special challenges, post-game content.

Anyone who earns every trophy should feel they truly got to know the game.

---

### Rule 4 — Variable trophy count

There is no mandatory fixed count. General reference:

> **15 to 40 trophies per game.**

Smaller games may have smaller lists. Large or highly varied games may exceed it. The count is determined by the game's relevant content, never by an artificial target.

---

### Rule 5 — Difficulty distribution

| Tier | Approximate share |
|---|---:|
| 🥉 Bronze | 50% |
| 🥈 Silver | 30% |
| 🥇 Gold | 15% |
| 💎 Platinum | 5% |

These are references, not math to be satisfied exactly.

- **Bronze** — early progress, discoveries, first contact with systems, simple side objectives, natural milestones.
- **Silver** — deeper exploration, intermediate challenges, quest chains, relevant side content, partial mastery.
- **Gold** — major challenges, advanced content, hard optional bosses, large collections, significant mastery, standout objectives.
- **Platinum** — awarded automatically when every other base-game trophy is earned.

---

### Rule 6 — Story trophies are allowed

Chapters, bosses and major events may generate trophies. But a trophy is not created for every mandatory chapter or boss just to inflate the list. Story trophies should mark significant milestones of the journey.

---

### Rule 7 — Optional content must carry real weight

Significant side content belongs in the list: major side quests, optional bosses, secret areas, relevant minigames, parallel systems, important collectibles, post-game content.

The more central a piece of content is to the game's identity, the stronger its presence in the list should be.

---

### Rule 8 — Collectibles in moderation

Collectibles may generate trophies when they matter for exploration or progression. One possible progression:

> First collectible found → Bronze. A significant amount → Silver. Full collection → Gold.

This structure is never applied automatically, and redundant trophies for the same collection are not created just to grow the list.

---

### Rule 9 — Cumulative trophies require tracking

Cumulative objectives may exist only when the game lets the player clearly check progress.

**Valid:** *Collect every Power Moon in a region.*
**Invalid:** *Defeat 1,000 Goombas* — with no counter available.

---

### Rule 10 — No artificial or tedious challenges

Avoid objectives such as: finishing the game with the starting weapon only, standing still for a set period, repeating the same mission dozens of times, grinding with no meaningful benefit, or performing arbitrary actions purely to unlock a trophy.

Exceptions exist when such a challenge genuinely fits the game's design or culture.

---

### Rule 11 — Maximum difficulty is not automatic

Difficulty-based trophies are used only when the game has meaningful difficulty modes, the difficulty significantly changes the experience, the added challenge is fair and interesting, and completion is clearly verifiable.

---

### Rule 12 — Speedruns are not automatic

Time-based objectives may exist when the game officially encourages speedruns, rankings, medals, records or timed challenges. Arbitrary time requirements are not invented for games with no meaningful relationship to that kind of challenge.

---

### Rule 13 — Multiplayer and online handled with care

Avoid trophies heavily dependent on online servers, matchmaking, active communities or unknown players. Local or online multiplayer may generate trophies when it is central to the experience.

The list must remain reasonably achievable in the future.

---

### Rule 14 — DLCs get separate lists

The base game has its own Platinum. Relevant DLCs and expansions may receive additional lists that never interfere with the original Platinum.

---

### Rule 15 — Missable trophies must be flagged

Any objective that can become impossible to complete during a playthrough is clearly marked:

> ⚠️ **MISSABLE**

Where possible the description should convey the risk without unnecessary spoilers.

---

### Rule 16 — Spoilers are controlled

Trophies tied to major story events may use hidden names, hidden descriptions or secret-trophy status, to protect anyone reading the list before or during a first playthrough.

---

### Rule 17 — Mandatory trophy structure

Every trophy has:

- **🏆 Name** — thematic, tied to the game or the achievement. Always in English.
- **📝 Description** — the objective condition. Always in English.
- **🎖️ Tier** — Bronze, Silver, Gold or Platinum.
- **🏷️ Type** — story, exploration, challenge, collection, sidequest, boss, mechanic, minigame, postgame, multiplayer.
- **🔍 Verification** — how the player confirms the condition was met (see Rule 1).
- **🧬 Origin** — see Rule 23.

And where applicable: ⚠️ **MISSABLE** or 🔒 **SECRET**.

---

### Rule 18 — The Platinum needs its own name

Every game gets a thematic Platinum name.

> 💎 **The Legend Is Complete** — Earn every other trophy.

The generic label "Platinum" is never used as the name.

---

### Rule 19 — The Retrospective Test

Looking at the completed list should read as a retrospective of the journey. The player should be able to recall: *I explored that region. I beat that boss. I finished that quest. I mastered that system. I found that secret. I overcame that challenge.*

If the list tells the story of the player's experience, it did its job.

---

### Rule 20 — Relevant Completion

Pantheon officially uses the **Pantheon Model of Relevant Completion**. The Platinum does not necessarily mean completing 100% of all existing content. It means having lived, explored and mastered everything relevant to that game's experience.

Therefore:

- Hundreds of redundant items are not required simply because they exist.
- Excessive grinding to max out levels, gear or currency with no meaningful experiential gain is not required.
- Every repetitive variation of the same activity is not required when a representative amount already demonstrates mastery.
- Unique, meaningful content takes priority.
- Relevant optional bosses, important secret areas, major side quests and meaningful alternate endings are considered.
- Core systems are represented.
- 100% may be required when it is fun, reasonable, verifiable and coherent with the game's design.

The central question:

> **"Did a player who earned all of these trophies live everything worth living in this game?"**

If yes, the list is complete.

---

### Rule 21 — Randomness dependency

RNG-dependent trophies may exist when they represent central experiences and have a reasonable probability of occurring during a normal completion run.

Not allowed: extremely specific combinations of random events, excessive repetition to manipulate RNG, constant game or save resets to force outcomes, waiting disproportionate to the rest of the list, or ultra-rare events that do not represent relevant content.

Whenever an objective depends on RNG, evaluate: relevance to the game's identity, reasonable probability during a normal run, potential waiting time, legitimate in-game methods to improve the odds, and impact on total Platinum time.

> **"Does this trophy encourage the player to experience a relevant system, or does it just force them to wait for luck?"**

If it depends mainly on luck, excessive waiting or repeated RNG manipulation, remove or reformulate it.

---

### Rule 22 — Port Mode

When a native achievement list exists on another platform, that list becomes the starting material. Port Mode runs four passes, in order:

**1. Import** — Capture the complete native list from the source platform: name, description, and tier if the source provides one.

**2. Filter** — Remove every entry that violates Rules 1–21. A native list is reference material, not scripture. If a 63-achievement Steam list contains 22 grind entries, the Pantheon list ships with roughly 40 and that is the correct outcome. The filter is the product.

**3. Prune** — Remove every entry referencing content absent from the target version: unported DLC, missing online modes, cut features, platform-exclusive hardware gimmicks. **This pass is mandatory.** Skipping it produces impossible trophies that a player only discovers after dozens of hours.

**4. Complete** — Add trophies for content exclusive to the target version: extra characters, added modes, exclusive areas, changed endings.

Then recategorize and rename:

- Sources without tiers (Steam, GOG, most storefronts) require tiers assigned under Rule 5.
- Sources with tiers (PlayStation, some Xbox structures) keep them unless the target version changes the difficulty.
- Names are rewritten as thematic English names under Rule 17, keeping `origin_id` for traceability.

The resulting list is validated against all rules like any other.

---

### Rule 23 — Provenance

Every trophy records where it came from:

| `origin` | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `original` | Created by Pantheon |
| `native-steam` | Adapted from a Steam achievement |
| `native-playstation` | Adapted from a PlayStation trophy |
| `native-xbox` | Adapted from an Xbox achievement |
| `native-other` | Adapted from another storefront or platform |

Entries adapted from a native list also record `origin_id` — the original achievement name — so any claim can be traced back to its source.

Provenance exists to separate what is verified fact from what is editorial judgment. In Genesis Mode every trophy is `original`. In Port Mode, a list where nothing is `original` did no Complete pass, and a list where nothing is native probably ignored its own source.

## 🔎 Creation Process

Before a list is created, analyse:

1. Main campaign structure
2. Core mechanics
3. Progression systems
4. Side content
5. Exploration structure
6. Existing collectibles
7. Mandatory bosses
8. Optional bosses
9. Secret areas
10. Minigames
11. Post-game content
12. Difficulty modes
13. Multiplayer systems
14. Statistics and available ways to track progress
15. Missable content
16. DLCs and expansions
17. RNG-dependent systems and reasonable event frequency
18. Version differences between the target platform and others

Only after this analysis is the list created.

---

## 🧪 Validation Checklist

**Automated** — enforced by `scripts/validate.mjs`: schema conformance, ID format and uniqueness, exactly one correctly positioned Platinum with a proper name, valid tiers and types, tier distribution against Rule 5, trophy count against Rule 4, verification present on every entry, missable and secret flags present, provenance consistent with the build mode.

**Judgment** — requires an auditor:

- Are all trophies factually accurate for this game and platform?
- Any imaginary counters?
- Is the campaign represented without artificial inflation?
- Is the most relevant side content present?
- Are core systems and mechanics covered?
- Are there interesting challenges beyond natural progression?
- Any excessively repetitive objective or unnecessary grinding?
- Are collectibles used in moderation?
- Are missable trophies flagged and spoilers protected?
- Does the list work as a retrospective of the journey (Rule 19)?
- Does the Platinum respect Relevant Completion (Rule 20)?
- Do RNG objectives have reasonable probability, without excessive waiting (Rule 21)?
- In Port Mode: was the Prune pass actually performed against the target version?

If every answer is satisfactory, the list is approved.

---

## 💎 Final Principle

> **A good trophy list does not exist to force the player to do everything.**
>
> **It exists to encourage the player to experience everything that makes that game worth playing.**
