## Notes

Genesis Mode — Nintendo-exclusive, no native achievement list exists on Wii U, Switch, or Switch 2. At the user's explicit request, the official paid Expansion Pass is included as two `dlc`-style bonus lists (Rule 14: The Master Trials, The Champions' Ballad), never affecting the base Platinum. Content is treated as identical between Switch and Switch 2 — a dedicated re-verification pass confirmed no Switch-2-exclusive gameplay feature exists (only performance improvements, a second save slot, and a companion smartphone app), unlike Metroid Prime 4: Beyond's genuine hardware-exclusive control scheme, so no separate Switch-2 bonus pack was built. Initial research hit a shared search-tool budget exhaustion that left several facts unresolved or conflicting; a follow-up re-verification pass resolved nearly all of them, including two corrections to the original research brief: the Champions' Ballad's four per-character questlines are given by Sheikah Monk Monuments via quests named 'EX Champion [Name]'s Song', not by the champion descendants (Teba/Riju/Yunobo/Sidon, who are flavor-only NPCs); and Eventide Island's actual base-game challenge is the 'Stranded on Eventide' shrine quest (place 3 Ancient Orbs), not a chest-hunting NPC as originally assumed. Exact Hyrule Compendium and Korok Seed totals are well-confirmed (385/394 entries, 900 seeds) but the Compendium menu itself shows no numeric readout, so its trophy verifies via visual full-completion instead of a cited number. Whether an in-game 'Divine Beasts freed' counter exists could not be resolved even after re-verification (the two re-verification passes disagreed with each other), so that trophy instead verifies via all four Champion abilities appearing in the item menu, which is confirmed. Post-audit fix (round 1): the Korok Seed trophy originally required all 900 seeds, but the dossier confirms only 441 are needed to fully expand every Weapon/Bow/Shield inventory slot — the remaining ~459 have no mechanical benefit, and collecting all 900 is a well-known, self-aware joke reward in the game's own design (Hestu's Gift). 'Full Stock' (Silver) now caps at the 441-seed practical threshold. Post-audit fix (round 2): 'Uncharted No More' originally required 100% on the map completion percentage, but that stat is 72%-weighted toward Korok Seeds, meaning literal 100% is mathematically impossible without all 900 seeds — this silently reintroduced the exact grind 'Full Stock' was fixed to avoid, so citing the percentage as 'the game's own metric' wasn't sufficient justification on its own (an auditor correctly rejected this reasoning in round 2). 'Uncharted No More' was rebuilt around discovering every named location on the world map instead — a genuinely explorative goal, directly verifiable (every location name is revealed on the map, no percentage or Korok count involved), with no grind-for-its-own-sake component.
