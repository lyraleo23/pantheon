## Notes

Genesis Mode — Nintendo Switch/Switch 2 have no native achievement system and Super Mario Bros. Wonder has never released on any platform that has one, so no port source exists. Target SKU is 'Super Mario Bros. Wonder – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Meetup in Bellabel Park' (worldwide March 26, 2026), which bundles the original 2023 campaign with new Switch 2-exclusive content. Several factual figures are only medium/low-confidence community estimates that could not be cross-verified against an official Nintendo source (total Wonder Seed count, exact per-world course counts, exact combined badge total for the Switch 2 Edition, exact Toad Brigade rank ladder) — no trophy description states these exact numbers; every collection trophy instead points to the game's own medal/checkmark tracking, which is confirmed to exist regardless of the precise underlying count. A community-sourced claim of a 7th save-file medal for Bellabel Park completion (found only in a single unverified forum thread) was deliberately excluded per user confirmation during the dossier checkpoint — too weak a source to build a trophy on. A checkpoint decision initially placed the Koopaling/Brigade Tent boss encounters in the base list, on the assumption they were reachable from the base-game world map independent of Bellabel Park. Two audit rounds corrected this: round 1 found Ludwig alone requires Bellabel Park; round 2 found that entering Bellabel Park is itself the prerequisite that unlocks all Brigade Tents (the opening cutscene for all seven Koopalings plays on first entering the park), so every Koopaling encounter — not just Ludwig's — depends on owning the paid content. All Koopaling-related trophies (first defeat, full sweep, Bellabel Flower recovery, remixed rematches) now live exclusively in the 'Meetup in Bellabel Park' DLC block; the base Platinum rests only on content confirmed free of Bellabel Park (the four Wonder Bowser Jr. palaces and Castle Bowser). A second audit round also found that a trophy for 'Rosalina & Co-Star Luma using Joy-Con 2 mouse controls' described a joint control state that does not exist — Rosalina is a standalone playable character and Co-Star Luma is a separate second-player assist slot, never combined the way the trophy described — so that trophy was removed rather than risk a second inaccurate rewrite; the Switch 2 Edition's roster/control additions are still represented indirectly through the game's other multiplayer and mechanic trophies. SMBW-022's ('Sound Off', formerly SMBW-024) verification checks the 24 base badges directly via the Badge menu rather than the save-file Badge medal, since it's unconfirmed whether that medal's threshold changed to include the Switch 2 Edition's new Dual/Support badges. No permanently missable content was found — all courses, bosses, and shops remain replayable/revisitable at any time. The DLC pack requires the paid Switch 2 Edition or its separate Upgrade Pack; it is not available to owners of the original 2023 Switch cartridge without upgrading.
