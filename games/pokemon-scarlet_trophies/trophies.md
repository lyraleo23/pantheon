### `PKS-025` 🥇 Pokémon Professor

Complete the base game's Paldea Pokédex, before installing any DLC.

**Tier:** 🥇 Gold · **Type:** Collection

**How to verify:** The Shiny Charm is granted by Jacq, confirming every non-Mythical base-game Pokédex entry has been registered as caught.

> The Paldea Pokédex expands to include Teal Mask/Indigo Disk species once that DLC is installed, which would pull paid DLC content into a base-game trophy's requirements (Rule 14) if pursued out of order. Complete this trophy — and claim the Shiny Charm — before installing either DLC pack; the Charm persists afterward even as the Pokédex grows. Separately, the Pokédex includes Miraidon's slot, Violet's exclusive legendary — completing the dex requires trading for one from another player's Violet copy (or a second personal copy), the same category of external dependency this list already excludes Palafin's Union Circle evolution for under Rule 13. This is standard, unavoidable series behavior rather than a design flaw, but the player should know going in rather than discover it at the very last entry.

## Notes

Genesis Mode. Nintendo Switch has no native achievement/trophy system, and Scarlet has never released on any other platform. Scope decisions made at the research checkpoint: (1) The Hidden Treasure of Area Zero DLC gets two separate trophy lists under `dlc` — The Teal Mask and The Indigo Disk — per Rule 14, matching the precedent from Hyrule Warriors, Breath of the Wild, and Pokémon Legends Z-A earlier in this project; neither affects the base Platinum. (2) The base-game Paldea Pokédex's exact catchable total is disputed across sources (400 vs. 373) and whether the game displays an explicit completion percentage could not be confirmed, so PKS-025's trophy text avoids citing a specific number — verification instead uses the Shiny Charm, a discrete, unambiguous reward item only granted on full Pokédex registration, sidestepping the uncertainty entirely. (3) Mystery Gift-based Pokémon and expired real-world-time-limited Tera Raid event bosses (e.g. past 7-star event distributions) are excluded entirely from this list, not merely missable-flagged — these were time-gated promotional content spanning 2022-2024 that is almost certainly closed by now, and building a trophy around content a current player likely cannot access at all would violate Rule 13's 'reasonably achievable' standard even more directly than an online-only trophy would. (4) The Union Circle-gated Finizen-to-Palafin evolution (requires another player connected) is deliberately NOT used in any trophy per Rule 13, since it's the one Pokédex-relevant mechanic in the base game dependent on multiplayer and cannot be guaranteed available to a future solo player. (5) Team Star's five bosses are correctly NOT treated as Scarlet-exclusive (they're identical in both versions bar cosmetics) — only Great Tusk (replacing Violet's Iron Treads) as the Quaking Earth Titan is a genuine version-exclusive mandatory boss. (6) A note for the record: the original research brief referenced a character named 'Clive' — no such character exists in this game; the AI professor is Sada (Scarlet) / Turo (Violet). No trophy references this name.
