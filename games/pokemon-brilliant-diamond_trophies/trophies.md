## Notes

Genesis Mode. Nintendo Switch has no native achievement/trophy system, and Brilliant Diamond has never released on any other platform. No paid DLC or expansion pass exists for this game — no `dlc` block. Scope decisions made at the research checkpoint: (1) Manaphy, Shaymin, and Darkrai were all real-world time-limited Mystery Gift distributions (Nov 2021-Apr 2022) with no confirmed re-run since, and are excluded from this list entirely as permanently expired — unlike the other three Mythicals. (2) Mew (PBD-023), Jirachi (PBD-024), and Arceus (PBD-029) are each gated behind linked save data from another specific game (Let's Go Pikachu/Eevee, Sword/Shield, and a completed Legends: Arceus save respectively) but have NO time limit — included at explicit user request, who confirmed owning all three prerequisites; each is marked missable with a notes field disclosing the dependency. Mew and Jirachi share the same tier (Silver) since both are functionally identical NPC handouts gated purely on detecting linked save data, with no completion requirement for either linked title; Arceus alone sits at Gold because it requires a fully completed Legends: Arceus save, a materially bigger ask. (3) PBD-027 (National Dex completion) verifies via the Shiny Charm reward; note that in this specific game the Shiny Charm only boosts breeding shiny odds, not wild-encounter or Poké Radar odds (a documented quirk), so no trophy claims otherwise. (4) The classic Gen IV Underground flag-capture minigame was confirmed REMOVED in this remake — no trophy references it. (5) No trophy requires Global Wonder Station (Wonder Trade) or any online/multiplayer feature, per Rule 13 — these were also confirmed added post-launch (v1.3.0), not present at initial release. (6) Trophy Garden's rotating roster is confirmed NOT version-exclusive (identical content between Brilliant Diamond and Shining Pearl, save-file/time-based) and is not used as a version-exclusive trophy hook; Ramanas Park's Johto/Rainbow Room (Raikou/Entei/Suicune/Ho-Oh) is the genuine, confirmed Brilliant-Diamond-exclusive collection hook used instead (PBD-028). (7) PBD-017 (first Ramanas Park catch) is tiered Silver rather than Bronze and positioned after the pre-Hall-of-Fame Silver trophies (badges, commanders, lake trio), since Ramanas Park only unlocks after National Dex + Hall of Fame — placing it earlier would misrepresent the list as a chronological retrospective (Rule 19).
