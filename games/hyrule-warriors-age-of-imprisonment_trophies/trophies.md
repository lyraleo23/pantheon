## Notes

Genesis Mode — Switch 2-exclusive, no native achievement list exists anywhere. This game only has FREE post-launch content updates, not paid DLC, so no `dlc` bonus list is included (unlike Hyrule Warriors and Breath of the Wild earlier this session, both of which had real paid Expansion Passes). This was the newest and least-documented game researched this session; a proactive re-verification pass (after the initial 3-scout research hit severe tool-access friction) resolved the full 19-character playable roster, the 6-chapter/28-battle campaign structure, and a boss-naming correction — the initial brief assumed 'Archfiend' was an official 4-member boss category, but it's actually just a one-time chapter title; the real recurring boss cluster is four 'Grim-'-prefixed named bosses (Grimgera, Grimtorok, Grimgohma, Grimgibdo). Two facts remain unconfirmed even after re-verification and are deliberately not assumed: whether an in-game Medal List/achievement system or an overall completion-percentage tracker exists (both lean absent but aren't conclusively proven so — no trophy depends on either). No trophy references a discrete numeric counter for roster recruitment or battle completion since none was confirmed to exist — 'recruit everyone'/'complete every battle' trophies instead verify via visual menu-completion state (no locked slots remaining). No trophy references Phantom Ganon or 'Forbidden Difficulty,' since these are tied to a second free content update whose release status as of the research date is unconfirmed — including them would risk citing content some players may not yet have. No trophy requires the cross-save bonus weapons (from Age of Calamity or Tears of the Kingdom save data) or amiibo, since both require external ownership beyond this game itself, consistent with the precedent set for similar content earlier this session.
