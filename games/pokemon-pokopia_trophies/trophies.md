## Notes

Genesis Mode — Pokémon Pokopia is Nintendo Switch 2 exclusive with no native achievement list on any platform. The game has no combat/battle system; there are no boss-type trophies. Several researched facts were left deliberately vague to avoid Rule 1/2 violations where sources conflicted: Environment Level's maximum value (5 vs 10 in different guides), the exact Pokédex species total (300 vs ~304), and the exact General Request count (~60 vs 74) are never hardcoded in a trophy description — trophies instead point at the in-game screen that shows completion. Shiny Pokémon were excluded entirely: official Pokémon.com community consensus says they don't exist, against one unreliable fan guide claiming a 1/500 rate. Time-limited real-world events (seasonal cosmetics, Mystery Gift codes) were excluded per Rule 13's 'reasonably achievable in the future' spirit, since a future player has no guaranteed way to earn them. The base list targets the game as of the free Ver. 2.0.0 update (Aug 5, 2026), which is now standard for all owners. The `dlc` list covers Expansion Pass Part 1 'Bubbly Basin' (released Aug 5, 2026) at the user's request; Parts 2 and 3 had not released as of this list's research date (Aug 12, 2026) and are not represented.
