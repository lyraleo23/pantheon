## Notes

Genesis Mode — Bayonetta 3 is a Nintendo Switch console exclusive; no other platform release exists (Nintendo funded development, as with Bayonetta 2), so there is no native achievement list to port. No DLC of any kind was ever released, confirmed via the complete official patch history (v1.0.0-v1.2.0). No content is permanently missable — every chapter, side mission, and secret chapter remains replayable via Chapter Select indefinitely. Per user confirmation at the dossier checkpoint, the Non-Stop ∞ Climax difficulty trophy was deliberately scoped to 'unlock the difficulty' rather than 'clear the full campaign on it,' because difficulty can be freely switched per chapter from the Chapter Select menu and no source confirms the game records a per-chapter 'cleared on hardest difficulty' flag — a full-campaign-on-hardest trophy could not be verified as genuine rather than gamed (Rule 11). Since the difficulty unlocks automatically the moment the story is finished on any difficulty, it would be redundant with the story-completion trophy (BAY3-024), so no separate trophy was created for it at all. Also per checkpoint confirmation, Card Packs (41, medium confidence) and Figure Boxes (total disputed between 43 and 46 across sources) have no in-game numeric or percentage tracker of any kind — only individual finds are confirmed visible via new Gallery art — so only bronze 'first find' trophies were built for these, with no 'collect them all' trophy that would rely on a disputed number and an unconfirmed aggregate tracker. An earlier research pass wrongly flagged Medallion Shards/the Golem secret boss as fabricated; this was independently re-confirmed as real by 6+ sources across two later passes and is included at full confidence. The Chapter 14 final boss's true identity is left unnamed in trophy text (Rule 16) to avoid spoiling the story's central twist. An audit pass caught a dossier error: Jeanne does NOT become playable through the 14 main story chapters after unlocking her post-game — she remains restricted to her own Side Missions, Phenomenal Remnant chapters, Witch Trials, and secret chapters; BAY3-022 was corrected accordingly. Umbran Tears of Blood are caught, not defeated in combat (BAY3-006 corrected), and the 14th Phenomenal Remnant unlocks via story completion rather than tear collection like the other 13 (BAY3-021 corrected). BAY3-025 was added to represent the Demon Slave giant-demon combat sequences, one of the game's most identity-defining systems, which the original draft omitted entirely.
