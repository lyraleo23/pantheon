## Notes

Port Mode from the PS4/PS5 list (35 trophies: 1 Platinum, 5 Gold, 8 Silver, 21 Bronze). The paramedic trophy name is spelled 'Savior' (gtabase/gta.wiki; PowerPyx has 'Saviour'). Conservative build at the player's request: the only in-game counters treated as confirmed are the Pause > Stats completion percentage plus the specific Stats rows the player accepted at checkpoint (Criminal Rating; Vice City media attention and property damage; San Andreas attribute bars and weapon skills) - those rows are medium-confidence, sourced from guides rather than a captured Definitive Edition screen; every native entry that depends on an unconfirmed cumulative counter was filtered (Rule 1-2). Several such activities remain covered implicitly by the 100% completion trophy, whose percentage IS shown in Stats. Mission-condition trophies are verified by what the player observes before the Mission Passed screen; mission replay for passed story missions is unconfirmed for the Definitive Edition (one guide says none exists), so those are flagged MISSABLE as the safe assumption. Story mission totals are disputed across sources, so no trophy cites a mission count. Social Club accomplishments quoted by gtabase/gta.wiki are not console-native and were not imported. The Switch has no native trophies, so cheats have no gating effect here; the list assumes a cheat-free save. CJ's attribute bars (fat, respect, sex appeal) and weapon skill levels are read from the Stats menu (medium confidence, accepted at checkpoint). Collectibles (tags, snapshots, horseshoes, oysters) have no dedicated trophy: no counter is confirmed and Remastered (100%) already requires them. Ain't Nothing But a G Thing is a judgment call kept on the strength of the territory map, the game's own turf display. Local 2-player co-op from the original release is absent from the Definitive Edition; no trophy references it. Gang turfs captured early are lost when CJ first leaves Los Santos and must be retaken after the return, which is why Ain't Nothing But a G Thing is effectively post-story. Validator warning on Bike or Biker is a false positive: it is a single run whose checkpoints are shown on screen, not a cumulative total.
