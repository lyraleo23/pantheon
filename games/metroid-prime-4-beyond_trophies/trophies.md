## Notes

Genesis Mode — Nintendo-exclusive, no native achievement list exists on either Switch or Switch 2. Several disputed dossier facts were deliberately excluded or left unquantified to avoid Rule 1/2 violations: exact collectible totals (Missile Expansions 46 vs. 48, Energy Tank/Shot Expansion/Green Energy Crystal totals) are contested across sources and never cited — full-collection trophies (Fully Loaded, Living Archive, Full Bloom) verify against the game's own confirmed percentage/meter readouts instead. The exact named list of missable Logbook scans is contested between guides, so no trophy names a specific missable scan; missability is instead flagged generally on the trophies that require pre-endgame collection, matching the dossier's confirmed hard point-of-no-return before the final Sylux fight. 'Fusion Mode'/Fusion Suit was not confirmed to exist in this title (likely conflated with Metroid: Samus Returns) and is excluded entirely. At the user's request, amiibo-unlocked content and the Nintendo Switch 2-exclusive Joy-Con 2 Mouse Mode feature are collected into a separate `dlc`-style bonus list ('Switch 2 & amiibo Extras') rather than the base list, since they require owning specific physical merchandise or Switch 2 hardware — per Rule 14 this bonus list never affects the base game's Platinum, which remains earnable by any player on either platform with no additional purchases.
