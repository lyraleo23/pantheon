## Notes

Genesis Mode. Neither the original Wii release (2011) nor the Switch HD remaster (Skyward Sword HD, 2021) has ever had a native achievement/trophy system, and no other platform release exists. Base list represents the original Wii-era content; per explicit user decision at the research checkpoint, Switch-exclusive material (button controls, free camera, item wheel, faster loading, skippable cutscenes, and the amiibo-gated Zelda & Loftwing fast-travel feature) is entirely QoL/convenience with no new areas, items, quests, or bosses, and was deliberately dropped rather than built into a bonus/DLC section — there is no `dlc` block. Hero Mode (the game's New Game+) is not required for the Platinum: it is a full second playthrough of the entire campaign under harder rules, and requiring it would mean beating the whole game twice for one trophy, which Rule 20 (Relevant Completion) counsels against — a player who has earned every other trophy has already lived everything the base campaign has to offer. A full 'collect all 27 Goddess Cubes' trophy was deliberately excluded: research found no in-game tracker (numeric or otherwise) for cube/chest completion, only the first-cube moment is verifiable as a discrete event, so only that is represented (Rule 2). Side quests are represented via two individually named, individually verifiable quests rather than a single 'complete every side quest' trophy, because Skyward Sword has no in-game quest log or Adventure Log of any kind — total side quest count is not even consistently documented across sources, let alone tracked by the game itself.
