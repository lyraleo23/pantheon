## Notes

Genesis Mode. Switch has no platform-level achievement system, and no evidence was found of Game Center/Google Play Games achievement integration for HOME on mobile either — this is an absence-of-evidence conclusion (medium confidence), not an explicit official denial. Scope decisions made at the research checkpoint: (1) A LARGE share of HOME's feature set — all trading (GTS, Wonder Box, Room Trade, Friend Trade), the friend list, mobile's reward-bearing 'Challenges' and 'Medals' systems, and Battle Data — is confirmed MOBILE-APP-ONLY and does not exist on the Nintendo Switch build at all, per Nintendo's own support FAQ. No trophy anywhere in this list requires any of these. (2) All Premium Plan-gated content (the Judge/IV-checker function, Pokémon Bank transfer, storage above the Basic Plan's 30-Pokémon cap, and the higher Wonder Box/GTS/Room Trade limits) is deliberately excluded from every trophy, base or DLC, so that the Platinum never requires a paid subscription — the same principle this project applies to online-service dependencies under Rule 13, extended here to a recurring-payment gate rather than a server dependency. (3) A full National Pokédex (1,025 species) completion trophy was deliberately NOT built: like the National Dex problem already identified for this project's FireRed/LeafGreen Switch list, completing it requires owning and trading across virtually every mainline Pokémon game plus the mobile-only trading suite, making it unreasonable under Rule 20. The base list instead uses partial milestones (30/100/150 species registered) that are genuinely trackable and achievable, and each DLC pack's Pokédex trophy is scoped to that one game's own regional dex instead. (4) Several HOME-exclusive Mystery Gift rewards tied to regional-dex completion (Shiny Manaphy, Enamorus, Keldeo, Volcanion, the Legends Z-A starter trio) had conflicting research on whether they are currently live or already expired as of October 2026 — every regional-dex trophy in this list is therefore worded around the Pokédex completion itself (a stable, confirmed, in-app fact) rather than asserting the bonus gift is currently claimable. (5) Pokémon Champions, Pokémon Bank, and Pokémon GO are explicitly out of scope at the user's direction: Champions is a battle/tournament spin-off with no exploration or regional Pokédex of its own, unlike the other seven connected games, so it gets no DLC pack; Bank (a one-way legacy 3DS bridge sunsetting Feb 2027) and GO (whose transporter lives on the mobile app, not Switch) are mentioned here for context only, with no trophies built around either. (6) FRLGH-004 (the Celebi gift) is included despite requiring the companion mobile app for delivery — the qualifying action happens on Switch, but the gift itself is mobile-only — because this project's precedent (Pokémon Brilliant Diamond, Pokémon Legends: Arceus) already discloses rather than silently excludes this kind of external dependency; it is clearly flagged in that trophy's notes. A first-draft trophy for the Legends: Z-A Alpha starter trio Mystery Gift was removed after audit: unlike Celebi, that gift's current live/expired status is itself disputed in research (see dossier conflicts), so rather than layer a mobile-dependency disclosure on top of an unresolved availability question, it was dropped entirely — the Legends: Z-A pack rests on its two unambiguous hooks (first transfer, Research Tasks, regional dex) instead. (7) Every DLC pack in this list is intentionally thin (3-4 trophies) relative to the validator's 5-trophy guidance: HOME's actual game-specific hooks are limited to a confirmed, small set of real mechanics (first transfer, that game's 20-task Research Task set, that game's regional Pokédex reward, and at most one further confirmed quirk unique to that connection) — inflating any pack further would mean inventing content the research never found, which Rules 1-2 forbid.
