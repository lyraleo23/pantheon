## Notes

Genesis Mode — Nintendo-exclusive, no native achievement list exists on Switch or Switch 2. Confirmed no paid DLC was ever released for this game (unlike Breath of the Wild's Expansion Pass), so no `dlc` bonus list is included. Content is treated as identical between Switch and Switch 2 — confirmed no exclusive gameplay feature exists on Switch 2, only performance improvements, a second save slot, and a companion app, consistent with the Breath of the Wild precedent. Two proactive re-verification passes were run before design (mirroring the successful approach used for Breath of the Wild) after initial research hit the same shared search-tool budget exhaustion; nearly every load-bearing fact was resolved to medium/high confidence — see dossier.json's `reverifiedFacts`. One important departure from Breath of the Wild: this game DOES have real missable content (three one-time bosses that cannot be rematched — Moragia, Sludge Like, and Master Kohga after his side adventure — plus non-replayable quests and non-respawning chests). Only Master Kohga's fight is represented as a trophy (TOTK-014, flagged missable) since it's tied to clearly-optional side content; Moragia is mandatory main-quest content so is not flagged missable despite also being unrematchable, and Sludge Like was excluded entirely due to insufficient confirmed detail about its context. The Korok Seed trophy is capped at 421 seeds (the confirmed practical threshold for maxing all inventory slots) rather than the full 1,000 available, applying the same Rule 20 lesson learned from the Breath of the Wild audit: the remaining ~579 seeds have no further mechanical benefit. Exact totals for the Hyrule Compendium (509), map completion percentage weighting, and Autobuild schematic cap remain unconfirmed or only medium-confidence, so no trophy cites those specific numbers — verification relies on visual/menu completion state instead. Moragia's exclusivity to the Goron questline (vs. an equivalent existing for other regions) is not explicitly confirmed by any source, only inferred from absence of evidence — trophy text avoids claiming this exclusivity outright. Post-audit fixes: added TOTK-032/TOTK-033 covering the Dragon's Tears/Geoglyphs system (12 memories forming the game's central narrative mystery), which the first audit pass correctly flagged as a major representation gap — Rule 3 requires the list to represent the game well, and this is arguably its most identity-defining piece of content. Also corrected a reversed item name ('Light of Blessing', not 'Blessing of Light') and removed two unsupported embellishments from verification text (an invented 'All's Well' reward item name, and a fabricated Koltin completion-confirmation claim) that the dossier did not actually support.
