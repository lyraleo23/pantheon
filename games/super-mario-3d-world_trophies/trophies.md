## Notes

Genesis Mode — this list targets the original 2013 Nintendo Wii U release specifically, WITHOUT the 2021 Switch 'Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury' additions (no Bowser's Fury campaign, no online multiplayer, no amiibo support of any kind). Two initial scope assumptions were corrected during scouting: Toad has always been playable on Wii U (it is Toadette who has never been playable in any version), and the Koopalings do not appear in this game at all — its bosses are original/returning characters (Boom Boom, Pom Pom, Hisstocrat, Boss Brolder, King Ka-thunk, Prince Bully, Motley Bossblob) plus three Bowser fights. Several disputed dossier facts were deliberately left unquantified to avoid Rule 1/2 violations: the exact total course count (116 vs. 117 across sources), exact Green Star/Stamp/gold-flag thresholds to unlock post-game worlds, and whether a single aggregate collectible counter exists anywhere — full-collection trophies instead verify against the World Map/Course List per-level state and the save-file's own 5-star completion rating, which is a real, directly-displayed in-game system. The full list of secret exits could not be confirmed beyond two known examples, so 'Shortcut!' only requires finding one, not all. The Miiverse Stamp-posting feature is permanently non-functional since Miiverse's 2017 shutdown and is not referenced by any trophy; Stamps themselves remain fully collectible and trackable. No DLC was ever released for the Wii U version.
