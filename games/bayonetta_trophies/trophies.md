## Notes

Port Mode — sourced from the PS3 native trophy list (51 trophies: 1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 10 Silver, 38 Bronze; Xbox 360 shares the same 50 base achievements). IMPORT: all 51 native entries captured (dossier_scout_native.json). FILTER: dropped 10 granular chapter-range completion trophies (Chapters 1-4/5-7/8-11/12-13/14-Epilogue on Normal and Hard) as redundant slices of Umbra Witch/Umbra Elder/Taste Of The Witching Hour (Rule 20). Also dropped 12 combat-technique trophies whose native condition is a cumulative action count (e.g. 50 Torture Attacks, 20 Wicked Weaves with Dodge Offset, 10 consecutive Witch Time engagements, 10 airborne Kulshedra grabs, 20 enemies frozen with Odette, 20 Concoctions crafted) because the PS3/Xbox achievement systems track these invisible, silently, in the OS — Bayonetta's own in-game UI exposes no equivalent counter for any of them (confirmed via the official Wii U manual's full menu documentation), so a player on Wii U has no way to verify the condition was met (Rule 1/2). The two Alfheim-discovery trophies (discover all 21 / discover half) were dropped for the same reason — no confirmed in-game portal checklist exists — while the Gold trophy for CLEARING all 21 Alfheim challenges was kept, since its completion is independently verifiable through a real, confirmed event: the Lost Chapter: Angel Slayer becoming selectable in Chapter Select. One near-duplicate pair (Nice And Relaxed / Touch And It Will Hurt, both about the Moon of Mahaa-Kalaa accessory) was consolidated by keeping only the 3-consecutive Silver variant, verifiable in the moment without any counter. One trivial one-off (Tread Not So Softly, jump-kill any enemy) was dropped as an arbitrary action with no mastery value (Rule 10). Treasure Collector and Treasure Fanatic were reworded to reference the confirmed, combined Umbran Tears of Blood total (101, tracked directly by the Rarities file per the official Wii U manual) rather than an unconfirmed 'resting places' sub-count, after an audit round caught that distinction wasn't actually supported by any source. PRUNE: no content needed pruning — every scout confirmed the Wii U port is a strict content superset of PS3/360/Steam (extra costumes, Japanese dub, GamePad features), never a subset, so nothing on the native list references content missing from Wii U. COMPLETE: added one original trophy (A Witch of Two Worlds) for the four Nintendo-themed costumes exclusive to the Wii U/Switch releases (Peach, Daisy, Samus, Link), each with real gameplay-affecting quirks, not just cosmetic swaps. Whether Fox McCloud also belongs to this game (rather than being Bayonetta 2-exclusive) was investigated across three separate research passes because sources disagreed. It was ultimately settled against inclusion: a directly-fetched Siliconera article quoting PlatinumGames' own costume announcement states plainly that the Wii U port of the original Bayonetta adds costumes for Peach, Samus, and Link only, and that Fox McCloud is added specifically 'in Bayonetta 2' — a more direct and authoritative source than the ambiguous Wikipedia sentence an earlier pass had relied on. The five boss-defeat trophies were kept at their native Bronze tier (single, self-evident events, no counter needed) per Rule 22's guidance to preserve native tiers unless the target version changes difficulty, which it does not. Per the user's explicit decision, Nintendo Switch differences (an amiibo-scan shortcut that instantly unlocks the same costumes already purchasable with Halos, and nothing else) are documented here rather than built into a separate DLC-style list — there isn't enough distinct Switch-exclusive content to justify one. Two trophies carry lower-confidence wording flagged by research: 'Treasure Collector'/'Treasure Fanatic' verification is inferred to route through the same Rarities file screen that tracks Umbran Tears of Blood, not independently confirmed as a separate screen; and the exact on-screen name of the hardest difficulty (rendered here only as 'the hardest difficulty' rather than quoting a specific string, since sources disagreed between '∞ Climax' and 'Non-Stop Infinite Climax'). One source claimed roughly 15 trophies are marked secret on the native PS3 list, but which specific entries could not be retrieved; only Master Of The Heavens (the Father Balder reveal) is marked secret here, as the one entry with an unambiguous story-spoiler surface — the remaining story trophies (A Primer In The Magical Arts, A Mother's Love, Just In The Nick Of Time, Naughty Tentacles) describe early or mid-game scenes without revealing a plot twist, so they are left visible rather than speculatively hidden.
