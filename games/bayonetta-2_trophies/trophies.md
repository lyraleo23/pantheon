## Notes

Genesis Mode — no native achievement list exists for this game on any platform (Wii U has no achievements, and the 2018 Nintendo Switch re-release has none either). No DLC was ever released for Bayonetta 2 (confirmed by director Yusuke Hashimoto at E3 2014 — everything, including all Nintendo-themed costumes, ships on-disc), so unlike some other titles there is no separate DLC list here. Per the user's explicit decision, Nintendo Switch differences are documented here rather than built into a separate bonus list, since neither difference is substantial enough to justify one on its own: (1) amiibo scanning is Switch-exclusive but only as a shortcut to unlock costumes already purchasable in-game with Halos — no new content; (2) Tag Climax (the online co-op/competitive mode) gained local wireless 2-console co-op on Switch, where the Wii U original was online-only — a genuine functional difference, but a connectivity feature rather than new unlockable content. Wii U's Tag Climax also depended on Nintendo Network, which was permanently shut down April 8, 2024, so no trophy in this list depends on live online play. Several native-feeling collectible systems were deliberately excluded from cumulative-count trophies because their exact totals and in-game tracking UI could not be confirmed with confidence for this specific game (as opposed to Bayonetta 1, where the equivalent systems were confirmed via the official manual): the ~50 'Umbran Tears of Blood' (20 Umbran Crows + ~30 Bewitchments) and the Muspelheim hidden-verse total are represented here only through single-occurrence 'first' trophies, which are self-evident in the moment and don't require an unconfirmed aggregate counter. The Rodin secret weapon (obtained via an extreme Halos grind, reported around 9,999,999) is deliberately excluded from the weapon-collection trophy for the same reason Mario Kart 8's Gold Glider was excluded from that list: it represents grinding disproportionate to any meaningful gameplay gain, not mastery of a core system. Jeanne and Rosa's playable-character unlocks are not given separate trophies since they occur automatically at the same milestones already covered by existing trophies — Jeanne on completing the story at any difficulty (BAY2-008) and Rosa on completing it at Hard (BAY2-010).
