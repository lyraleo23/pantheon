## Notes

Genesis Mode — Switch-exclusive, no native achievement list exists anywhere. At the user's explicit request, the official paid Expansion Pass is included as two `dlc`-style bonus lists (Rule 14: Pulse of the Ancients, Guardian of Remembrance), never affecting the base Platinum, matching the pattern used for Hyrule Warriors and Breath of the Wild earlier this session. This research round required a proactive re-verification pass (3 scouts) plus one additional narrow follow-up pass (specifically on mission-replay mechanics) after the initial pass and first re-verification both hit search-tool budget exhaustion — more research friction than any other game this session. Two corrections to the original research brief were caught: this game has NO grid-based Adventure Mode (all side content lives on a single Breath-of-the-Wild-style world map instead), and Master Kohga is base-game content while Sooga is the DLC-exclusive character (the initial assumption had this backwards). The narrow follow-up pass confirmed Legend Mode scenarios are replayable via a 'Select Scenario' menu and that missed Korok Seeds remain retroactively collectible (persistent per-scenario state) — so the Korok Seed trophy is safely designed as a lifetime pursuit rather than a risky one-shot. It also surfaced a Medal List gallery (Gallery menu, shows collected/uncollected Medals) that the first re-verification pass had incorrectly concluded probably didn't exist. Several facts remain genuinely unresolved even after two re-verification rounds and are deliberately NOT cited in any trophy: the exact Korok Seed total (134 vs. 175, likely different counting methodologies, not a true conflict, but neither confirmed as the 'right' number to use), the exact Challenge total, the DLC quest-count breakdown by wave (internally contradictory across sources), and Monk Maz Koshia's playability (rests on a single uncorroborated source, so no trophy names him). No difficulty-mode trophy references the DLC-exclusive Apocalyptic difficulty — the base list's difficulty trophy uses Very Hard, the hardest difficulty available without the Expansion Pass.
