## Notes

Genesis Mode. Nintendo Switch has no native achievement/trophy system, and Sword has never released on any other platform. Design decisions made at the research checkpoint: (1) Sword's version-exclusive Gym Leaders (Bea, 4th; Gordie, 6th) are deliberately NOT given individual trophies — they play an identical structural role to Shield's Allister/Melony, so a dedicated trophy would inflate the list without adding meaningful distinct content; they're covered by PSW-003 (first badge) and PSW-014 (all 8 badges), mirroring the Scarlet list's precedent of not trophying Great Tusk individually despite it being version-exclusive. (2) The Isle of Armor and Crown Tundra expansions each get a separate trophy list under `dlc` per Rule 14; neither affects this base Platinum, matching the precedent from the Scarlet list's Teal Mask/Indigo Disk lists. (3) PSW-026 (Pokémon Professor) targets only the base 400-species Galar Pokédex, deliberately completed before either DLC pack is installed (the Galar Dex does not expand from the DLC packs — they have their own separate dexes — but Zamazenta, the Shield-exclusive version legendary, and several other version-exclusive species still require trading with another player/copy to complete even the base dex; this is standard series behavior, not a design flaw). (4) Two scout sources disagreed on whether the Pokédex screen shows a numeric completion percentage on Switch; PSW-026 sidesteps this entirely by using the Shiny Charm — a discrete, unambiguous key item — as its verification target instead. (5) PSW-024 (Warrior of Legend)'s description deliberately avoids naming an exact catch location for Zacian, since sources disagreed on whether it's caught at Hammerlocke Stadium or the Slumbering Weald. (6) Battle Tower trophies (PSW-018, PSW-027) are phrased as one-time 'reach' milestones rather than 'maintain,' sidestepping a source disagreement over whether Battle Tower rank can regress after a loss. (7) Battle Stadium (online ranked/casual battles, requires paid Nintendo Switch Online) is deliberately excluded from this list per Rule 13 — Max Raid Battles and the Battle Tower are both confirmed fully soloable offline and are used instead. (8) The large batch of 2020-2022 time-limited Mystery Gift/serial-code event Pokémon (Shiny Zacian/Zamazenta, Zarude, Shiny Zeraora, etc.) is excluded entirely, not merely missable-flagged, since their distribution windows are almost certainly closed for a player starting today — the same reasoning the Scarlet list applied to its own expired event content. (9) List size and tier proportions (13 bronze / 8 silver / 6 gold / 1 platinum = 27+1) intentionally mirror the Scarlet list's structure, since both are similarly-scoped single-region mainline entries.
