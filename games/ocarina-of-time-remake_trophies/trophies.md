## Notes

Genesis Mode. The 2026 remake is a Switch 2 exclusive and Switch 2 has no system-level achievements, so no native list exists anywhere. Research caveat: Zelda Wiki and Nintendo's own pages were unreachable to every scout; facts come from guide and review sites (medium confidence). The player does not own the game yet and asked, at the checkpoint, to include only what research could confirm. Consequently the following were deliberately left out until they can be verified for the remake: the Biggoron's Sword trading sequence, Big Poe hunting, dedicated Fishing Pond and minigame trophies (fishing prize thresholds conflict between sources), the Gerudo training ground (name and reward unverified), Magic Beans and the mask quest. Collection trophies are anchored to in-game rewards (House of Skulltula gifts, the life meter, Quest Status song staff, bottle slots, Collection screen) (item-get messages, the life meter, the pause-menu songs page) rather than to per-region map counters or named menu screens, whose existence on the console is unconfirmed. Validator warnings on OOT-018/019/020/021 (cumulative without a named counter) are accepted: each is confirmed by a discrete in-game event — the HUD life meter showing 20 hearts, and the House of Skulltula handing over its 20-, 30- and 100-token rewards, which it only does once the threshold is reached — so the player never has to count manually. ZELDA NOTES (smartphone app) is never used as verification because it is not part of the game. Master Quest, Hero Mode, New Game+ and DLC do not exist in the remake at launch. amiibo-gated masks are excluded because they require extra hardware. Hall of Fallen Foes trials (Stone, Silver, Gold) are a remake addition; their exact unlock trigger conflicts across sources, so trophies describe the trial, not the unlock route. Revision after audit round 1: the Great Fairy trophy was dropped (count and menu conflict between sources: 3 vs 6 rewards), and the all-bottles trophy was dropped because one bottle requires the excluded Big Poe hunt. Side-content coverage is knowingly thin; this follows the player's checkpoint instruction to include only confirmed content. The list should be revised (listVersion 1.1) once the remake can be checked first-hand: Epona and Lon Lon Ranch, minigames, the Biggoron trade, Great Fairies, Big Poes and the Gerudo Fortress are the expected additions. Audit round 2 found that OOT-018 (20 hearts) and OOT-021 (100 tokens) depend on fishing, minigame and Magic Bean content excluded elsewhere; the player chose to keep both full collections and accept that dependency, so the Platinum does include that content indirectly. Status set to approved on that player decision after the second revision round, per the pipeline's two-round limit.
