## Notes

Genesis Mode — unified list covering both the Nintendo Wii U original (2014) and the Nintendo Switch 'Definitive Edition' (2018) at the user's explicit request, since neither platform has native achievements and this game never released anywhere that does. The BASE trophies list targets the Wii U game and is earnable with zero additional purchases on either platform (a Switch Definitive Edition owner already has everything the base list needs, since Definitive Edition includes the Wii U base game). Additional content layers on as five `dlc`-style bonus lists (Rule 14, never affecting the base Platinum): the four real, historical Wii U paid DLC packs (Master Quest Map Pack, Twilight Princess Pack, Majora's Mask Pack, Boss Pack — each independently purchasable on Wii U, each pre-included at no cost on Switch Definitive Edition), plus a fifth 'Definitive Edition Exclusives' pack for content that originated in the 3DS spin-off Hyrule Warriors Legends and was carried into Switch Definitive Edition — this content does not exist on Wii U in any form, even with all four DLC packs purchased. Cia, Volga, and Wizzro became playable via a FREE Wii U software update (not paid DLC) and are treated as base-list roster/content, since that patch is standard and long since part of any current copy of the game. Several disputed dossier facts were deliberately left unquantified to avoid Rule 1/2 violations: exact Legend Mode scenario titles beyond the two independently-confirmed ones ('The Armies of Ruin', 'A War of Spirit'), exact Gold Skulltula totals and the unreconciled Apothecary threshold, and exact Medal List totals per version are never cited — trophies instead verify against the actual in-game screens (Adventure Map grid, Gallery > Medal List, Bazaar illustration progress) that are confirmed to exist. No trophy in the base list requires amiibo, since Wii U's Spinner-weapon amiibo requirement does not exist on Switch and amiibo ownership cannot be assumed; the single amiibo-related trophy in the Definitive Edition Exclusives bonus pack is explicitly optional bonus content and does not gate the base Platinum.
