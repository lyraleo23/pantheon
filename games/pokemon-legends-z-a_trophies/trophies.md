## Notes

Genesis Mode. Neither Nintendo Switch nor Switch 2 has a native achievement/trophy system, and no other platform release exists. Game content and story are confirmed identical between the Switch and Switch 2 Editions (only performance/resolution differ), so this list applies to either. Revised after audit: an initial draft included three trophies built around a 'Zygarde Cell' collectible system — that mechanic does not exist in this game (it was confused with the Sun/Moon-era Zygarde Cell hunt); Legends Z-A catches and completes Zygarde in a single scripted postgame boss fight instead, so those trophies were removed/reworked. The initial draft also placed 'Rogue Mega Rush' (which requires the paid Mega Dimension DLC, since its 27-fight roster includes the DLC-exclusive Mega Zeraora) in the base list, which would have made the base Platinum depend on paid DLC in violation of Rule 14 — it has been moved into the Mega Dimension DLC list instead. Other scope decisions: (1) Mewtwo and Diancie ARE included (PLZA-023, PLZA-024) despite both requiring a real-world, time-distributed Mystery Gift code to access — an explicit user override of the precedent set earlier in this project (comparable amiibo-gated content was dropped from Skyward Sword); both are marked missable with notes on the external-code dependency. (2) The Mega Dimension paid DLC gets its own trophy list under `dlc`, per Rule 14 and explicit user request; it never affects the base Platinum. (3) The postgame character 'L' is deliberately NOT named as Lysandre in any trophy text — that identity comes only from low-confidence community plot summaries, not confirmed in-game dialogue. (4) Mega Pidgeot, despite heavy pre-release marketing, is confirmed to be a backstory-only event, never a player-controlled battle — no trophy is built around it. (5) 'A Request from the Rust Syndicate' and 'An Invitation from the SBC' are mandatory main-story missions, not optional side quests (corrected from an initial mistyping) — they're typed `story`, and the Boutique Couture unlock (defeating designer Asami while the player's own Pokémon is Mega Evolved — corrected from an initial description that had this backwards) is folded into the SBC mission trophy rather than double-counted as its own entry. (6) The exact base-game side mission total is inconsistent across sources (119-121), so no trophy references a specific side-mission count. (7) No multiplayer/Link Play trophies are included per Rule 13, since online functionality cannot be guaranteed available to every future player. (8) PLZA-027 (Pokédex completion) is marked missable: a low-confidence report suggests at least one required species may have only a single non-repeatable encounter in the entire game — flagged as a caution rather than asserted as fact, since it wasn't independently confirmed. (9) A second audit round found the Mega Dimension DLC's actual endgame extends well past Mega Darkrai: a targeted follow-up scout confirmed defeating Darkrai only rolls the DLC's story credits and opens a further mandatory postgame chain — Primal Groudon and Primal Kyogre (Mission 12's continuation, gated behind Hyperspace survey-point thresholds), then a two-phase Mega Rayquaza fight at the Hyperspace Sky Pillar, which community sources treat as the DLC's true climax (though no distinct in-game 'True Ending' cutscene or menu flag could be confirmed, so that specific terminology is avoided in trophy text). The DLC list was expanded from 12 to 16 trophies to cover Rogue Mega Heatran (the mandatory pre-Darkrai boss), Groudon, Kyogre, Rayquaza, and the hidden 'Raging Lightning' side mission that actually unlocks Mega Zeraora (previously described only as 'obtain the stone' without its real, substantial unlock chain). The Rogue Mega Rush trophy's verification was corrected to cite the confirmed 'Mega Rush Master' title reward rather than a vague 'completion reward.' Gold-tier weighting in the DLC list (8 of 16) is intentional for the same reason as the base list: this expansion's entire purpose is its endgame content, and trimming it would mean cutting the content players bought the DLC for.
