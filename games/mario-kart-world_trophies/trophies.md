## Notes

Genesis Mode — Nintendo Switch 2 has no native achievement/trophy system, and Mario Kart World is a Switch 2 exclusive with no release (and therefore no native list) on any other platform. Knockout Tour is a live-service feature: it launched with 8 rallies and has grown to 12 as of update v1.8.0 (Sept 10, 2026) via free content updates, with more plausible in the future. The 'gold trophy in every Knockout Tour rally' trophy deliberately reads 'every current rally' rather than a hard-coded number so it does not break as the roster grows; the count is pinned here for context only. No missable content was found anywhere in the game — every collectible, unlock, and mode is permanently available via Free Roam replay, VS Race, or mode select at any time, so no MISSABLE flags are used. Free Roam's collectible totals (394 P-Switch missions, 200 Peach Medallions, 150 ?-Panels — 744 combined) and the 1,056-entry sticker book are deliberately NOT required in full under Rule 20: thresholds (300 P-Switch missions, 75 ?-Panels, 100 Peach Medallions, 500 stickers) represent substantial, representative engagement with Free Roam without demanding exhaustive completionism, some of which requires advanced techniques (Wall Runs, Rail Rides) on a handful of P-Switches. Full-collection trophies are reserved for the character roster (50) and vehicle roster (40), both moderate, game-defining, non-grindy sets comparable to Mario Kart 8's 'Full Roster' and 'Fully Loaded' trophies. Costume/outfit totals conflict between sources (103 vs. 127), so the costume trophy uses a fixed absolute threshold (20) rather than a fraction of an unverified total. No dedicated lifetime-stats screen (total races, distance, win rate) was confirmed to exist, so no trophy references such a counter directly; cumulative trophies instead rely on menus and counters that were confirmed (cup trophy displays, Free Roam per-category collectible counters, the sticker book, and the roster/vehicle select screens). Online trophies are deliberately modest (a single race win, completing one Knockout Tour) with no VR/rating thresholds, per Rule 13 — online servers are healthy as of September 2026, but a competitive rating climb is not something every future player could reliably achieve. Mario Kart World has no boss encounters, consistent with the entire series, and effectively no plot, so the Special Cup/ending trophy carries no spoiler risk and is not marked secret. No DLC exists as of September 2026, so Rule 14 does not apply and there is no separate dlc array. 200cc does not exist in Mario Kart World as of v1.8.0, so no 200cc-based trophy was designed; Mirror Mode is treated as the game's true hardest-difficulty unlock.
