## Notes

Genesis Mode — Switch exclusive, no native achievement list exists on any platform. Several disputed dossier facts were deliberately excluded or left unquantified to avoid Rule 1/2 violations: the post-game Hidden Theets hunt has no confirmed in-game counter and its total is contradictory across sources (70 vs. an internal ~46 vs. a separate ~16), so only a single 'find your first Theet' trophy exists rather than a 'collect all' trophy. Sparkle Gem-threshold-to-unlock-boss numbers are contradictory across sources and are never cited. Outfit/Collection totals (72 vs. an inconsistent 84-item breakdown from the same source) are disputed, so the Wardrobe Icon trophy points at the Collection menu's fully-filled state rather than citing a specific outfit count. No confirmed aggregate '% complete' stat exists anywhere in the game, so full-completion trophies (Gem Completionist, Backstage Pass, Picture Perfect, Wardrobe Icon) are verified via each relevant per-category screen showing no gaps remaining, not a single percentage stat.
