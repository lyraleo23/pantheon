## Notes

Genesis Mode — Wii U has no native achievement system, and Mario Kart 8 Deluxe (Switch) has no native list either, so no port source exists. Base list covers the vanilla Wii U base game only (8 cups / 32 tracks); both paid DLC packs get their own separate lists under `dlc` per Rule 14 and never affect the base Platinum. 200cc and Mirror Mode arrived via free post-launch updates (Ver. 4.0/4.1, April-May 2015) and are unlocked by default on any currently patched copy, so no trophy is built around 'unlocking' them — they're treated as simply two of the five available engine classes. All Nintendo Network online features (online races, VR/BR ratings, online win/loss tracking, Mario Kart TV online sharing) were permanently shut down April 8, 2024, and Miiverse (which displayed Staff Ghost stamps) shut down in November 2017 — no trophy depends on either. Amiibo-unlocked Mii Racing Suits are excluded as hardware/purchase-dependent (~19 suits requiring physical amiibo figures the player may not own). The Gold Glider vehicle part (10,000 lifetime coins, a purely cosmetic reward with no gameplay benefit) is deliberately excluded per Rule 20 — it represents excessive grinding without meaningful experiential gain, unlike the ~2,800-coin threshold for all standard parts, which is included. Battle Mode in the vanilla Wii U release is thin by design (only Balloon Battle, played on repurposed race tracks rather than dedicated arenas — the arena overhaul is exclusive to Mario Kart 8 Deluxe on Switch) and is represented by a single trophy rather than inflated with redundant Battle Mode content. Vehicle-part and coin-threshold figures (e.g. ~2,800 coins for all standard parts, Gold Standard/Gold Tires unlock conditions) are community-sourced (GameFAQs, SuperCheats) and cross-corroborated across independent research passes, but no official Nintendo total was found — treated as medium-confidence but design-safe since all are genuine persistent, visually-verifiable in-game unlocks regardless of the exact coin count.
